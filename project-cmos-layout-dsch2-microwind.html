VLSI Design Fundamentals Using DSCH2 and Microwind 3.1

CMOS gates, transmission gates and a ring oscillator, from schematic to layout.

The problem

A logic gate on paper is not a chip. This project followed the digital IC flow from circuit schematic through to physical layout.

What I built

  • Designed and simulated CMOS Inverter, NAND, NOR, XOR, Transmission Gates and a Ring Oscillator.
  • Performed schematic design in DSCH2 and CMOS layout generation in Microwind 3.1.
  • Followed standard CMOS design rules and checked functional behaviour.
  • Gained hands-on experience of the digital IC design flow, circuit design to physical layout.
