Design, Control and Implementation of Grid-Connected Renewable Energy System

SRF-PLL and SOGI-PLL grid synchronisation, inverter control and P&O MPPT, validated on Arduino Due and C2000.

The problem

A grid-connected inverter must lock onto the grid voltage and then inject controlled current while keeping the DC link steady. That chain, from PV to grid, has to work in simulation and on real hardware.

What I built

  • Integrated wind and solar energy conversion in a grid-connected system model.
  • Designed and implemented SRF-PLL and SOGI-PLL for grid synchronisation and compared them.
  • Built three-phase grid-connected inverter models with current controllers and a DC-link voltage controller.
  • Designed L/LCL filters and the DC-link capacitor.
  • Added PV-based grid integration through a boost converter with Perturb & Observe (P&O) MPPT.
  • Validated the complete system through MATLAB/Simulink simulation and hardware implementation on Arduino Due and a C2000 microcontroller.
