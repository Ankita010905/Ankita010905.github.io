About

Turning concepts into practical solutions

Most engineering students learn concepts. I enjoy turning them into working systems through research, simulation and hardware implementation.

I'm a final-year Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at NIST University with a strong interest in renewable energy systems, power systems, power electronics, VLSI design, embedded systems and control engineering.

In summer 2026 I completed the SURE research internship at IIT Hyderabad, working on renewable energy integration, DFIG-based wind systems and PLL-based grid synchronisation. It sharpened my understanding of power system modelling, control strategy and research method.

Before that, a VLSI and digital system design internship at CTTC (MSME), Government of India, gave me hands-on time with Verilog HDL, CMOS layout, FPGA fundamentals and EDA tools: Xilinx ISE, Intel Quartus, DSCH2 and Microwind.

I also earned the NPTEL Smart Grid: Basics to Advanced Technologies certification with Elite + Silver (80%).

I'm looking for entry-level roles in solar PV, power systems and renewable-energy integration, and I'm open to internships and research collaboration.
