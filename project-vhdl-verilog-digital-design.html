Digital System Design Using VHDL and Verilog

Adders, shifters, counters and more, verified with testbenches in Xilinx ISE and Quartus.

The problem

Digital hardware has to be described precisely and then proven correct in simulation before it goes anywhere near an FPGA or a layout.

What I built

  • Designed and implemented Full Adder, Half Adder, Multiplexer, Shift Register, Barrel Shifter, Johnson Counter, Up/Down Synchronous Counter and Tri-State Inverter.
  • Verified functionality with testbenches, RTL analysis and simulation waveforms in Xilinx ISE and Intel Quartus.
  • Built practical understanding of HDL-based design and FPGA-oriented development workflows.
