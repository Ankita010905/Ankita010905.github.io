Wind Turbine Monitoring System (Hardware + IoT)

Sensors and ESP8266 on a custom board, streaming wind-turbine power data to cloud dashboards.

The problem

Understanding how a small wind turbine performs needs real measurements of its power and energy output, visible remotely and in real time rather than read off a meter at the site.

What I built

  • Designed and assembled a custom PCB-based monitoring circuit on a universal board.
  • Integrated sensors with an ESP8266 for real-time data acquisition and wireless communication.
  • Built cloud dashboards on ThingSpeak and Blynk, plus web dashboards, for remote monitoring and visualisation.
  • Measured and analysed wind turbine power and energy generation data.
  • Applied IoT concepts to renewable energy monitoring and smart energy management.
