Performance Analysis of BLDC Motor Using Different Controllers

PI, Fuzzy-PI and ANN-assisted Fuzzy-PI speed control of a BLDC drive for EV applications.

The problem

Electric vehicle drives need fast, stable speed response under changing load. A fixed-gain PI controller can struggle when operating conditions move, so intelligent controllers are worth comparing against it.

What I built

  • Modelled a BLDC motor drive system in MATLAB/Simulink.
  • Implemented PI, Fuzzy-PI and ANN-assisted Fuzzy-PI speed controllers.
  • Evaluated speed response, torque characteristics, settling time and overshoot under different operating and load conditions.
  • Compared the controllers on response time, stability, robustness and efficiency.
  • Investigated intelligent control techniques for electric vehicle drive applications.
