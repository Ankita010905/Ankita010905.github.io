Hybrid Renewable Energy System with Intelligent Energy Management

PV + battery storage with a fuzzy-logic energy management system.

The problem

Solar generation rarely matches load demand. A battery helps, but only if the power flow between PV, storage and load is coordinated so the PV is fully used and the battery is charged and discharged in a controlled way.

What I built

  • A PV-based power conversion stage with MPPT to extract the maximum available solar power.
  • Battery energy storage connected through a bidirectional DC-DC converter for controlled charging and discharging.
  • An energy management system that decides power flow from PV generation, load demand and battery state of charge (SOC).
  • Investigating a fuzzy-logic EMS for smoother power-flow coordination.

Planned next

  • MATLAB/Simulink analysis under varying PV generation, load demand and battery SOC.
  • Low-voltage hardware implementation for practical validation.
